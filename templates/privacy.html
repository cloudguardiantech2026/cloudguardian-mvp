Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · OputGuard Technologies Ltd

Summary: CloudGuardian uses temporary, read-only access to scan your cloud environment for Cyber Essentials compliance. We do not store your cloud credentials, access keys, or infrastructure data beyond what is needed to generate your compliance report. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Who We Are

CloudGuardian is operated by OputGuard Technologies Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (Company Registration No. pending). We provide cloud compliance intelligence services to UK small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and IT professionals.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, OputGuard Technologies Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the CloudGuardian platform.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account connection data: AWS IAM Role ARNs and External IDs used to establish temporary read-only access to your cloud account. These are not access keys and cannot be used to make changes to your infrastructure.
  • Scan results: Technical compliance findings generated from your cloud environment, including resource identifiers, configuration states, and control pass/fail status. This data is stored temporarily to generate your evidence pack and compliance report.
  • Contact information: Email addresses provided when using the "Invite IT Provider" feature, used solely to send the dashboard invitation link.
  • Usage data: Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited, used for security monitoring and service improvement.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • AWS access keys or secret access keys
  • Azure client secrets or certificates
  • Personal data belonging to your employees, customers, or end users
  • The content of your cloud workloads, databases, or storage objects
  • Payment card information (billing is handled by third-party payment processors)

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To perform Cyber Essentials compliance scans against your cloud environment
  • To generate evidence packs and compliance reports for your use
  • To detect compliance drift between scan cycles
  • To provide guided remediation recommendations through our conversational interface
  • To send invited IT providers a dashboard access link on your behalf
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our platform

5. How Cloud Access Works

CloudGuardian uses AWS Security Token Service (STS) AssumeRole to access your AWS environment. This means:

  • A temporary session is created with credentials that expire automatically after one hour
  • Access is strictly read-only — CloudGuardian cannot create, modify, or delete any resources in your accountYour unique External ID ensures only CloudGuardian's platform account can assume the role
  • You can revoke access at any time by deleting the CloudFormation stack from your account

For Azure environments, access is granted via a read-only App Registration with the Reader role assigned at subscription scope. No client secrets are stored by CloudGuardian.

6. Data Retention

Scan results and compliance data are retained for the duration of your active session and for a limited period to allow you to retrieve your evidence pack. We do not permanently store detailed infrastructure findings beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

Server logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and operational purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data with:

  • Infrastructure providers: Our platform is hosted on Render.com and uses AWS infrastructure. These providers process data only as necessary to operate our services.
  • AI processing: Our conversational compliance assistant uses the Anthropic API. Scan findings provided to this service are used solely to generate compliance guidance and are subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose data if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

8. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction: You can ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: You can ask us to provide your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You can object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

CloudGuardian uses session cookies to maintain your connection state during an active compliance session. These cookies are essential for the platform to function and are deleted when you close your browser. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

10. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Our platform uses HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, and we follow security best practices including the principle of least privilege for all cloud access. Scan results are not permanently stored on our servers beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's supervisory authority for data protection matters.
