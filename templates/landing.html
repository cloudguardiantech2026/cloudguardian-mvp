Why Cyber Essentials matters

It's not just a certificate.
It's a business requirement.

🏛

Government Contracts

All UK public sector contracts handling personal data or sensitive information require Cyber Essentials. Without it, you cannot bid.

🛡

Cyber Insurance

Most UK cyber insurers now require CE certification as a condition of cover. Failing to certify can invalidate your policy.

🤝

Supply Chain Access

Large enterprise clients increasingly mandate CE for suppliers and partners. It's becoming a standard commercial requirement.

⚖

Legal Protection

CE certification demonstrates due diligence. In the event of a breach, it shows your organisation took reasonable precautions.

📈

23% Annual Growth

Cyber Essentials certifications grew 23% year-on-year. The market — and the mandate — is expanding rapidly.

⏰

Annual Renewal

Certification must be renewed every year. CloudGuardian monitors continuously so you're always ready — not scrambling at renewal time.

⚡

April 2026 Danzell Update — New Rules in Force Now

The latest Cyber Essentials update (Requirements v3.3, 'Danzell') made cloud services explicitly mandatory in scope. Any organisation using AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud cannot exclude these services from assessment. Multi-factor authentication on cloud accounts is now an automatic-fail criterion. CloudGuardian is fully updated for Danzell — most tools are not.
